Implementation Guide

Design Tokens (Use these exact colors):

Primary: #1B3B2B
Background: #FBF9F5
Teal: #2D6A6F
Accent: #7A8B79

Typography:

  • Headings: Playfair Display, Georgia, serif
  • Body: Lora, Georgia, serif

Navigation Links:

  • Riverside Reader: https://riverside-readers-app.vercel.app/
  • Ask Riverside: https://riverside-books-chatbot-khaki.vercel.app/
  • Riverside Shelves: https://riverside-inventory-app.vercel.app/
  • Riverside Press: https://riverside-social-app.vercel.app/

Behavior:

  • Click X button in full sidebar to collapse
  • Click logo in collapsed sidebar to expand
  • Smooth transition animation (300ms)
  • Current page highlighted with teal background
